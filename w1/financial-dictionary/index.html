Core Financial Terms for Disciplined Investors

Clear, practical definitions of fundamental market concepts designed to help individual investors evaluate asset classes, manage portfolios, and navigate capital markets with confidence.

Assets

Stock (Equity)

A security representing fractional ownership in a corporation, entitling the shareholder to a proportionate share of the company's assets and corporate earnings.

Typical Role:Capital appreciation & dividends
Primary Factor:Business operational performance
Assets

Bond (Fixed Income)

A debt instrument where an investor provides capital to an entity (government or corporate) that raises funds for a defined period at a fixed or variable interest coupon.

Typical Role:Income preservation & capital stability
Primary Factor:Interest rates and issuer solvency
Assets

ETF (Exchange-Traded Fund)

A pooled investment vehicle that holds a collection of stocks, bonds, or commodities, trading on standard stock exchanges similarly to an individual equity share throughout the session.

Typical Role:Broad sector or market diversification
Primary Factor:Underlying index constituent drift
Benchmarks

Market Index

A hypothetical portfolio of investment holdings representing a specific segment of the financial market, utilized by analysts and portfolio managers as a performance benchmark.

Typical Role:Relative performance comparison
Primary Factor:Market capitalization weighting rules
Mechanics

Liquidity

The ease and speed with which an asset can be converted into cash without triggering a significant change in its prevailing market price or suffering excessive slippage.

Typical Role:Portfolio flexibility and rebalancing
Primary Factor:Order book depth and daily volume
Mechanics

Commission

A service charge billed by an investment broker or platform in exchange for handling order execution, advisory operations, or administrative transaction processing.

Typical Role:Direct cost evaluation on trade frequency
Primary Factor:Fee structures and brokerage terms
Benchmarks

Volatility

A statistical measurement of the dispersion of returns for a given asset over a designated timeframe, often indicating the frequency and magnitude of market price movements.

Typical Role:Variance measurement and sizing inputs
Primary Factor:Market uncertainty and macroeconomic data
Mechanics

Asset Allocation

An investment approach that balances return expectations against portfolio exposure by dividing holdings among major asset categories such as equities, fixed income, and cash reserves.

Typical Role:Long-term portfolio structure
Primary Factor:Time horizon and volatility tolerance
Benchmarks

Capital Gain

An increase in the value of an asset or investment above the purchase price. The gain is realized when the asset is sold, or remains unrealized while position remains open.

Typical Role:Tax computation and overall portfolio yield
Primary Factor:Entry purchase price versus sale price
